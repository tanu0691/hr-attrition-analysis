# 📊 HR Analytics - Employee Attrition Analysis

## Overview
Analyzed 1470 IBM employees to find why they leave 
the company using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

## Tools Used
- Python, Pandas, NumPy
- Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Dataset
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition - Kaggle
- 1470 employees
- 35 features

## Key Findings
1. Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12% (1 in 6 leaving)
2. Young employees (25-35) leave the most
3. Sales & HR have highest attrition rate
4. Low job satisfaction = more attrition
5. Low salary employees leave more ($2500 vs $5000)
6. Poor work life balance = more leaving

## Business Recommendations
- Provide growth opportunities for young employees
- Improve work culture in Sales & HR
- Raise salaries for lower paid employees
- Offer flexible hours & remote work options
